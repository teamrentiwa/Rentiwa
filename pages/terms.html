About Rentiwa

Rentiwa is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace.

Rentiwa does not own, store, inspect, verify, deliver or rent any products.

Rentiwa only provides a platform that connects people who want to rent out their items with people who want to rent them.

Every rental agreement is made directly between the Owner and the Renter.

👤

User Responsibility

Every user is responsible for their own actions.

Users must provide accurate information while creating listings.

Users are responsible for verifying the identity of the other party before handing over any item or making any payment.

Important Safety Guidelines

Recommended Safety Checklist Before Every Rental

Before renting any product, always follow these essential safety practices:

  • ✓ Verify the person's Aadhaar Card or any Government ID.
  • ✓ Verify Address Proof whenever possible.
  • ✓ Meet in a public location.
  • ✓ Inspect the product carefully before accepting it.
  • ✓ Test the product before taking it.
  • ✓ Take clear photos and videos before and after handing over the product.
  • ✓ Discuss rental charges clearly before the deal.
  • ✓ Discuss the security deposit before the deal.
  • ✓ Exchange contact numbers.
  • ✓ Never rush into a rental.
  • ✓ Always communicate with the owner before meeting.
  • ✓ Ask every important question before renting.

Security Deposit

Rentiwa strongly recommends that owners collect an appropriate refundable security deposit before giving any product on rent.

The amount should depend on the value of the product.

The owner and renter should mutually decide the deposit amount.
