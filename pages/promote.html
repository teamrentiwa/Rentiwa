Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Rentiwa Featured Promotions

What are Featured Listings on Rentiwa? ▼
Featured Listings are high-visibility product cards displayed in the top ⭐ Featured Listings carousel on the Rentiwa homepage. They receive priority exposure over regular listings to attract more local renters in Narela.
Can I feature my product directly without admin approval? ▼
No. To maintain high quality and trust across Rentiwa, every promotion request is carefully reviewed by the administrator before going live.
How do I pay for promotion? ▼
Once you submit a promotion request, the Rentiwa admin will reach out via WhatsApp (+91 8510823577) or Phone to provide UPI payment options (UPI, Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay).
How long does approval take? ▼
Most promotion requests are reviewed and approved within 15 to 30 minutes during business hours.
What happens when my promotion expires? ▼
When the promotion duration ends (e.g. 24 hours, 5 days, or 30 days), your item automatically reverts to a standard listing without losing its reviews or details. You can request renewal anytime!
